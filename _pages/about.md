I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Electrical Engineering at Louisiana State University (expected 2027), working in the areas of **Motion Planning & Control** and **Reinforcement Learning**. My research focuses on developing motion planning and control algorithms for autonomous vehicles, spanning path generation, trajectory optimization, and motion control through both optimization-based and learning-based approaches.

Before joining LSU, I earned my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the Florida Institute of Technology (2019), and both my M.E. and B.E. from Shijiazhuang Tiedao University, China (2020, 2016).

I also bring industry experience from two roles. As an ADAS Planning & Control Engineer at **DESAY SV Automotive** (2022–2023), I developed motion planning and control algorithms for Automatic Parking Systems deployed in production vehicles. Earlier, as an Embedded Software Engineer at **Zhejiang Dali Technology Co., Ltd.** (2020–2021), I worked on motion control and embedded systems for mobile robots.

## Research Interests

- **Optimization-based trajectory planning**: OCP/NLP formulations, collision-avoidance reformulations (OBCA, corridor-based), warm-starting strategies
- **Learning-augmented planning**: reinforcement learning as a complement to optimization-based methods
